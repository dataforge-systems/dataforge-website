About

Why DataForge exists

DataForge Systems was founded by a technologist with over 15 years in government, military, and enterprise IT — including a decade managing software inventory and compliance in some of the most security-conscious environments in the world. DoD networks, air-gapped systems, and zero-tolerance compliance requirements.

That background is the foundation for everything we do. Our software solves problems we've personally fought with for years. Our AI work is grounded in practical, deployable solutions — not research experiments. And our consulting draws on hard-won experience navigating the gap between what technology promises and what mission-critical organizations actually need.

We're building a technology company that stays close to the ground — one that ships real products, takes on real problems, and earns trust by delivering results.
