Everything a reseller actually needs

Built by someone who understands the hustle. Every feature exists because real resellers need it.

🗺️

Live Sale Map

Community-powered yard sale finder with address, dates, photos, and descriptions. Filter by type, distance, and date.

🛣️

Route Optimizer

Hit multiple sales in the most efficient order. Build your route, start driving, and let Haul navigate you through every stop.

📷

Flip Check

AI-powered item identification with live eBay comps. See what similar items are currently listed for before you buy.

📦

Inventory Management

Track every item you buy with photos, purchase price, condition, and status. See your entire inventory at a glance.

🛒

Direct eBay Listing

AI writes optimized listings. Post directly to eBay with photos, item specifics, and shipping — without leaving the app.

⚡

Batch Listing

Scan 10 items at a yard sale and list them all to eBay at once. Review, edit, and post in a single batch.

🚗

Auto Mileage Tracking

GPS detects your drives automatically. Classify as business or personal. Calculate IRS deductions at the standard mileage rate.

🧾

Receipt Scanner

Snap a photo of any receipt. OCR extracts the total automatically. Track every business expense for tax season.

Tax Reports

IRS-ready mileage logs, expense reports, and profit & loss statements. Export as CSV or PDF. Everything your accountant needs.

Auto-Relist

Set items to automatically relist after 7, 14, or 30 days. Keep your listings fresh without lifting a finger.

📉

Auto Price Drop

Automatically lower prices on stale listings. Set your floor so items never go below your minimum. Sell faster.

✅

Sold Tracking

Haul detects when your eBay items sell and calculates your profit after eBay fees, shipping, and purchase cost automatically.
