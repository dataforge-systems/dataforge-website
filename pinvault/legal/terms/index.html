7. Authentication Scans

Authentication scans are guidance, not guarantees. Confidence levels (High, Medium, Low) are AI-generated estimates based on visual analysis. They are not definitive determinations of authenticity, and they should not be the sole basis for any purchase, sale, trade, or financial decision.

Counterfeit detection in Disney pins is genuinely difficult. Even expert human authenticators with the pin in hand can disagree. Our AI does its best with the image you provide, but it can be wrong — both ways. A "High" confidence result doesn't prove a pin is authentic; a "Low" confidence result doesn't prove it's a counterfeit.

For this reason:

  • We make no warranty as to the accuracy of any authentication scan result.
  • You agree not to rely solely on PinVault's authentication scans when making decisions to buy, sell, trade, or pay for a pin.
  • If a scan returns Medium or Low confidence, we provide self-verification tips. Use those, get additional opinions from experienced collectors, and make your own informed judgment.
  • You waive any claim against DataForge Systems based on a transaction outcome that turned out differently than an authentication scan suggested it might.
