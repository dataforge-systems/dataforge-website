Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

The short version: Plain Simple does not store your documents, images, or personal information. We never sell your data. Your scans are processed and immediately discarded.

Who we are

Plain Simple is a mobile application that helps you understand confusing documents by explaining them in plain English using AI. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

What we collect

We collect almost nothing. Here is the complete list of data Plain Simple stores:

  • Scan counter — a single number stored locally on your device tracking how many free scans you have used. This never leaves your device.
  • Subscription status — whether you are a free or Pro user, stored locally on your device.
  • Saved explanations (Pro users) — if you are a Pro subscriber, your explanation history is saved locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to any server.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not store your photos or document images
  • We do not store your document explanations on any server
  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not use advertising SDKs
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever
  • We do not require an account to use the app

How your scans are processed

When you scan a document, your image is sent securely over HTTPS to Anthropic's Claude AI API for processing. The explanation is returned to your device and displayed. The image is then immediately discarded — it is not stored by Plain Simple or on any server we control.

Anthropic processes your image solely to generate the explanation. Their data handling is governed by the Anthropic Privacy Policy.

Your document history (Pro users)

Pro subscribers can save and revisit their explanations. This history is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. It is never uploaded, synced, or accessible to us. If you delete the app, your history is permanently deleted.

In-app purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. All billing is managed through your Apple ID.

Important disclaimers

Plain Simple is an AI-powered tool designed to help you understand documents in plain English. It is not a substitute for professional advice. Please read the following carefully:

  • Not medical advice — Explanations of medical bills, prescriptions, insurance documents, or any health-related content are for informational purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.
  • Not legal advice — Explanations of contracts, leases, legal notices, or any legal documents do not constitute legal advice. Consult a licensed attorney for legal matters.
  • Not financial or tax advice — Explanations of tax forms, bank statements, financial documents, or investment materials are for general understanding only. Consult a qualified financial advisor or tax professional for financial decisions.
  • AI limitations — Plain Simple uses AI to generate explanations. While we strive for accuracy, AI can make mistakes. Always verify important information with the original source or a qualified professional.
  • No guarantee of accuracy — Explanations are provided as-is without warranty of any kind. DataForge Systems LLC is not liable for any decisions made based on the app's explanations.

Children's privacy

Plain Simple is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
